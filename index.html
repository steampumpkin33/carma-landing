Get It on Your Phone

Your mechanic in your pocket.
Always.

Add Carma to your home screen and it works exactly like a native app — even offline. No App Store needed. No download fees. Just tap and go.

🍎
iPhone (Safari)
Open Carma in Safari → tap the Share icon → select "Add to Home Screen" → tap Add.
🤖
Android (Chrome)
Open Carma in Chrome → tap the ⋮ menu → select "Add to Home screen" → tap Add. A proper app icon appears instantly.
💻
Desktop (Chrome / Edge)
Look for the install icon (⊕) in the address bar, or go to Settings → "Install Carma." Works offline too.
